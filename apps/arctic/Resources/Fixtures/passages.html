Passages between advertisements

By Alex Reader

Passage 1. Ferry Amber crossed slowly with more gulls than passengers. Radio Amber played the song it had played all summer. Baker Amber set out the last loaves and counted twice. Tide Amber turned before anyone on the quay noticed. Lantern Amber swung above the door of the chandlery. Morning Amber began with rain over the harbour.
